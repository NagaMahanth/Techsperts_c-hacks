12 · FREQUENTLY ASKED

You asked. We answered.

Is this scientifically accurate?

Every impact factor is drawn from WWF, FAO, UNEP, IEA, or ICAO reports. We round for readability but didn't invent anything. Full citations in the Sources section.

Do you save my data?

No. Nothing is stored. The receipt is generated client-side and vanishes when you leave the page. Privacy is a feature, not a disclaimer.

Why doesn't the receipt use my actual rupee prices?

Because rupees aren't the point. The receipt measures what rupees never show you — the environmental cost. That's the whole idea.

Can I share my receipt?

Not yet. A share-image generator is on the roadmap. For now, screenshots work — the visual design is built to look good in one.

What if I don't know my exact weekly numbers?

Estimate. The point isn't perfect precision — it's the shape of the week. Even a rough answer reveals the pattern.

Is this a one-time project or ongoing?

Built in 8 hours by TECH SPERTS at a hackathon. If it's picked up by a sustainability org, it becomes a product.
